# Bundled instrument samples

## Acoustic piano

Selected and lossily compressed samples from **Salamander Grand Piano v3**, recorded by Alexander Holm and remapped by kinwie. The source library is licensed under Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 Unported (CC BY 3.0). Source: https://github.com/sfzinstruments/SalamanderGrandPiano and https://archive.org/details/SalamanderGrandPianoV3. See `licenses/SalamanderGrandPiano-LICENSE.txt` and `licenses/SalamanderGrandPiano-README.md`.

This application uses three of the source's sixteen velocity layers, minor-third sampling, mono conversion, 44.1 kHz Vorbis compression, and seven-second excerpts. Those changes are made solely for responsive offline distribution.

## Rhodes electric piano

Selected and compressed samples from **jRhodes3d**, a 1977 Rhodes Mark I Stage 73 recorded by Jeffrey Learman. Samples are CC BY-NC 4.0 and are distributed here only as part of this free, noncommercial application. Source: https://github.com/sfzinstruments/jlearman.jRhodes3d. See `licenses/jRhodes3d-LICENSE.txt` and `licenses/jRhodes3d-README.md`. Commercial redistribution requires separate permission from the author.

This application uses three velocity levels where the source recording provides them, mono source recordings, 44.1 kHz Vorbis compression, and seven-second excerpts.

The warm organ contains no third-party recordings; it is a locally modeled tonewheel instrument.
